    Backup D

    Listening: Tom and Lisa

    Listen to the conversation again (Lesson 1, Activity 2). Is each sentence true or false?

      Questions

      Show the script

      Tom: What time do you usually wake up in the morning, Lisa?

      Lisa: I usually wake up at 7:00 a.m. How about you?

      Tom: I wake up at 6:30 a.m. What's the first thing you do when you wake up?

      Lisa: I go to the bathroom to wash my face and brush my teeth. What about you?

      Tom: I do the same! Then I have breakfast. I like cereal with milk.

      Lisa: Oh, I have toast and jam for breakfast. What do you do after breakfast?

      Tom: I get dressed for work and leave at 8:00 a.m.

      Lisa: I leave at 8:30 a.m. to catch the bus to the office. How do you go to work?

      Tom: I drive my car to work. It's easier for me.

      Lisa: That's nice. I take the bus because I don't have a car. Do you have a busy day at work?

      Tom: Yes, sometimes it's busy, but I enjoy my job. How about you?

      Lisa: My job is busy too, but I like it. I finish work at 5:00 p.m.

      Tom: That's the same time I finish. After work, I usually go to the gym.

      Lisa: I just go home after work and relax by watching TV.

      Tom: I watch TV too, but later in the evening.
